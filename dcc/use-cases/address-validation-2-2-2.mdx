## Guide

<Steps>
  <Step title="**Create a new customization**">
    Create a new [**customization**](https://admin.shopify.com/apps/dynamic-checkout-customizer/customizations).

    ![](/images/screenshot_13.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Choose the Bundle upsell**">
    Choose the **Bundle upsell \*\*option from Upsell section

    ![Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 17 50 35](/images/Screenshot-2026-10-08-at-17.50.35.png "Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 17 50 35")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Configure conditional experience**">
    Add one or more conditions.

    ![Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 17 50 19](/images/Screenshot-2026-10-08-at-17.50.19.png "Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 17 50 19")
  </Step>
  <Step title="Products & Unit of Discount">
    Choose products and add discounts 

    ![Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 17 51 13](/images/Screenshot-2026-10-08-at-17.51.13.png "Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 17 51 13")

    ![Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 17 52 05](/images/Screenshot-2026-10-08-at-17.52.05-1.png "Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 17 52 05")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Display Settings**">
    Select display settings like (**Alignment ,Border-radius, Inner spacing, Product card layout, Navigation Method** )

    ![Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 17 52 30](/images/Screenshot-2026-10-08-at-17.52.30.png "Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 17 52 30")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Set the priority**">
    Configure the Priority (0–100) to determine the execution order when multiple customizations are eligible.

    ![Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 17 53 21](/images/Screenshot-2026-10-08-at-17.53.21.png "Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 17 53 21")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Copy the Placement ID**">
    Open the **Placement** tab and copy the generated **Placement ID**. You’ll use this in the Shopify Checkout Editor.

    ![Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 17 53 34](/images/Screenshot-2026-10-08-at-17.53.34.png "Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 17 53 34")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Activate the customization**">
    Change the **Status** to **Active** to enable the customization.

    ![Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 17 52 57](/images/Screenshot-2026-10-08-at-17.52.57.png "Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 17 52 57")
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Checkout Editor">
    Go to your Shopify [Settings](https://admin.shopify.com/settings)**→ Checkout**, select your checkout profile, and click **Customize**.

    ![Screenshot 08](/images/screenshot_08.png "Screenshot 08")
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the app block">
    In the Checkout Editor, click **Add app block**.

    ![Screenshot 12](/images/screenshot_12.png "Screenshot 12")

    <Note>
      Make sure the app block is placed in the same checkout placement you selected in Dynamic Checkout Customizer.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure the app block">
    Select the **Checkout – Upsell Products** app block, drag it to the desired location, and paste the copied **Placement ID**.

    ![Screenshot 02](/images/screenshot_02.png "Screenshot 02")
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish your changes">
    Click **Save** in the Checkout Editor to publish the customization.

    ![Screenshot 02 Save Clicked](/images/screenshot_02_save_clicked.png "Screenshot 02 Save Clicked")
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  **Why does it matter?**

  Address Validation helps reduce shipping errors by validating customer address input during checkout. This can reduce shipping and logistics costs by **up to 10%** while improving delivery accuracy and customer satisfaction.
</Note>
