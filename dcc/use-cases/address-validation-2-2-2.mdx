How to Upsell Products on Checkout using the **Template ->** **Upsell -> Bundle upsell** option? A simple walkthrough. 

<iframe src="https://drive.google.com/file/d/1VLz73JBkACvzaKPQkVLnkbmrjHWvVpMu/preview" width="640" height="480" allow="autoplay" />

## Guide

<Steps>
  <Step title="**Create a new customization**">
    Create a new [**customization**](https://admin.shopify.com/apps/dynamic-checkout-customizer/customizations).

    ![Image](/images/image-46.png "Image")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Choose the Bundle upsell**">
    Choose the **Bundle upsell** option from Upsell section

    ![Bundle 4 Upsell Card](/images/bundle_4_upsell_card.png "Bundle 4 Upsell Card")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Configure conditional experience**">
    Add one or more **conditions** to control when the customization is displayed during checkout.

    ![Bundle 1 Conditional Experience](/images/bundle_1_conditional_experience.png "Bundle 1 Conditional Experience")
  </Step>
  <Step title="Products & Unit of Discount">
    Choose products and add discounts 

    ![Bundle 7 Heading Selection Discount](/images/bundle_7_heading_selection_discount.png "Bundle 7 Heading Selection Discount")

    ![Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 17 51 13](/images/Screenshot-2026-10-08-at-17.51.13.png "Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 17 51 13")

    ![Bundle 8 Bundle Discount](/images/bundle_8_bundle_discount.png "Bundle 8 Bundle Discount")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Display Settings**">
    Select display settings like (**Alignment ,Border-radius, Inner spacing, Product card layout, Navigation Method** )

    ![Bundle 2 Display Settings](/images/bundle_2_display_settings.png "Bundle 2 Display Settings")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Set the priority**">
    Configure the Priority (0–100) to determine the execution order when multiple customizations are eligible.

    ![Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 17 53 21](/images/bundle_6_priority.png "Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 17 53 21")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Copy the Placement ID**">
    Copy the generated **Placement ID** from clipboard icon . You'll use this in the Shopify Checkout Editor.

    ![Bundle 5 Placement Id](/images/bundle_5_placement_id-1.png "Bundle 5 Placement Id")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Activate the customization**">
    Change the **Status** to **Active** using toggle button to enable the customization.

    ![Bundle 3 Active Bar](/images/bundle_3_active_bar.png "Bundle 3 Active Bar")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Preview**">
    Preview shows , How this block looks like at **CheckOut.**

    ![Bundle Preview Focus](/images/bundle_preview_focus.png "Bundle Preview Focus")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Save changes**">
    ![Bundle 9 Unsaved Changes](/images/bundle_9_unsaved_changes.png "Bundle 9 Unsaved Changes")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Open the Checkout Editor**">
    Go to your Shopify [Settings](https://admin.shopify.com/settings)**→ Checkout**, select your checkout profile, and click **Edit**.

    ![Screenshot 08](/images/screenshot_08.png "Screenshot 08")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Add the app block**">
    In the Checkout Editor, click **Add app block**.

    ![Screenshot 12](/images/screenshot_12.png "Screenshot 12")

    <Note>
      Make sure the app block is placed in the same checkout placement you selected in Dynamic Checkout Customizer.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Configure the app block**">
    Select the **Checkout – Upsell Products** app block, drag it to the desired location, and paste the copied **Placement ID**.

    ![Screenshot 02](/images/screenshot_02.png "Screenshot 02")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Publish your changes**">
    Click **Save** in the Checkout Editor to publish the customization.

    ![Screenshot 02 Save Clicked](/images/screenshot_02_save_clicked.png "Screenshot 02 Save Clicked")
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  **Why does it matter?**

  Address Validation helps reduce shipping errors by validating customer address input during checkout. This can reduce shipping and logistics costs by **up to 10%** while improving delivery accuracy and customer satisfaction.
</Note>
