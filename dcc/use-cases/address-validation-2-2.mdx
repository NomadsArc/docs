How to display a **Swap Variant** for each cart item on the Checkout using the **Template/Custom ->** **Cart Item Details -> Swap variant** option.

<iframe src="https://drive.google.com/file/d/1VLz73JBkACvzaKPQkVLnkbmrjHWvVpMu/preview" width="640" height="480" allow="autoplay" />

## Guide

<Steps>
  <Step title="**Create a new customization**">
    Create a new [**customization**](https://admin.shopify.com/apps/dynamic-checkout-customizer/customizations).

    ![Image](/images/image-45.png "Image")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Choose the Remove option**">
    Choose the **Swap Variant **option from Cart Item Details section

    ![Swap 6 Cart Item Details](/images/swap_6_cart_item_details.png "Swap 6 Cart Item Details")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Configure display condition**">
    Add one or more **conditions** to control when the customization is displayed during checkout.

    ![Swap 7 Conditional Experience](/images/swap_7_conditional_experience.png "Swap 7 Conditional Experience")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Select Swap Variant**">
    Select Swap Variant from Action Section to enable the relevant action.

    ![Swap 3 Swap Variant](/images/swap_3_swap_variant.png "Swap 3 Swap Variant")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Set the priority**">
    Configure the Priority (0–100) to determine the execution order when multiple customizations are eligible.

    ![Swap 2 Priority](/images/swap_2_priority.png "Swap 2 Priority")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Copy the Placement ID**">
    Copy the generated **Placement ID** from clipboard icon. You'll use this in the Shopify Checkout Editor.

    ![Swap 1 Placement Id](/images/swap_1_placement_id.png "Swap 1 Placement Id")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Activate the customization**">
    Change the **Status** to **Active** using toggle button to enable the customization.

    ![Swap 4 Active Bar](/images/swap_4_active_bar.png "Swap 4 Active Bar")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Preview**">
    Preview shows , How this block looks like at **CheckOut.**

    ![Swap Preview Focus](/images/swap_preview_focus.png "Swap Preview Focus")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Save the customization**">
    Click Save to store your configuration.

    ![Swap 5 Unsaved Changes](/images/swap_5_unsaved_changes.png "Swap 5 Unsaved Changes")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Open the Checkout Editor**">
    Go to your Shopify [**Settings**](https://admin.shopify.com/settings) → Checkout, select your checkout profile, and click **Edit**.

    ![](/images/screenshot_08.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Add the app block**">
    In the [Checkout Editor](https://admin.shopify.com/settings/checkout/editor), click **Add app block**.

    ![Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 16 32 10](/images/Screenshot-2026-10-08-at-16.32.10.png "Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 16 32 10")

    <Note>
      Make sure the app block is placed in the same checkout placement you selected in Dynamic Checkout Customizer.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Configure the app block**">
    Select the **Checkout – Edit cart item** app block, and paste the copied **Placement ID**.

    ![Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 16 32 42](/images/Screenshot-2026-10-08-at-16.32.42.png "Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 16 32 42")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Publish your changes**">
    Click **Save** in the Checkout Editor to publish the customization.

    ![Swap Clean](/images/swap_clean.png "Swap Clean")
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  **Why does it matter?**

  Address Validation helps reduce shipping errors by validating customer address input during checkout. This can reduce shipping and logistics costs by **up to 10%** while improving delivery accuracy and customer satisfaction.
</Note>
