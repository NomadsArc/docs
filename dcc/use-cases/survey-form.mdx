How to display **Survey Form** for the customers on the Checkout using the **Template ->** **Content -> Survey** option.

<iframe src="https://drive.google.com/file/d/1qjim-C2V6yShMHh57Hf9pkQ5qo1Vyf2n/preview" width="640" height="480" allow="autoplay" />

## Guide

<Steps>
  <Step title="**Create a new customization**">
    Create a new [customization](https://admin.shopify.com/apps/dynamic-checkout-customizer/customizations).

    ![Image](/images/image-39.png "Image")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Choose the Form type**">
    Select **Survey **from the available content types to display custom fields for survey during checkout.

    ![Template Content Card Survey Focus](/images/template_content_card_survey_focus.png "Template Content Card Survey Focus")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Configure display conditions**">
    Add one or more **conditions** to control when the form is displayed during checkout.

    ![Survey Conditional Experience Focus](/images/survey_conditional_experience_focus.png "Survey Conditional Experience Focus")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Configure form settings**">
    Configure the **Survey fields**.

    ![Survey Heading Options Focus](/images/survey_heading_options_focus.png "Survey Heading Options Focus")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Customize the display**">
    Configure the **Display Settings** to control how the survey fields will appear during checkout.

    ![Survey Display Settings Focus](/images/survey_display_settings_focus.png "Survey Display Settings Focus")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Activate the customization**">
    Change the **Status** to **Active** using toggle button to enable the customization.

    ![Survey Toolbar Focus](/images/survey_toolbar_focus.png "Survey Toolbar Focus")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Set the priority**">
    Configure the **Priority** (0–100) to determine the execution order when multiple customizations are eligible.

    ![Survey Priority Focus](/images/survey_priority_focus.png "Survey Priority Focus")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Copy the Placement ID**">
    Copy the generated **Placement ID** from clipboard icon. You'll use this in the Shopify Checkout Editor.

    ![Survey Copy Placement Id Focus](/images/survey_copy_placement_id_focus.png "Survey Copy Placement Id Focus")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Save the customization**">
    Click **Save** to store your configuration.

    ![Survey Unsaved Changes Focus](/images/survey_unsaved_changes_focus.png "Survey Unsaved Changes Focus")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Open the Checkout Editor**">
    Go to your Shopify [**Settings**](https://admin.shopify.com/settings) → Checkout, select your checkout profile, and click **Edit**.

    ![](/images/screenshot_08.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Add the app block**">
    In the [Checkout Editor](https://admin.shopify.com/settings/checkout/editor), click **Add app block**.

    ![Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 16 07 56](/images/Screenshot-2026-10-08-at-16.07.56.png "Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 16 07 56")

    <Note>
      Make sure the app block is placed in the same checkout placement you selected in Dynamic Checkout Customizer.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Configure the app block**">
    Select the **Checkout – Form Elements** app block, and paste the copied **Placement ID**.

    ![Image (4)](/images/image-\(4\)-1.png "Image (4)")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Publish your changes**">
    Click **Save** in the Checkout Editor to publish the customization.

    ![](/images/7894b79b54.png)
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  **Why does it matter?**

  [Sunco](https://www.sunco.com/) uses the **Notifications → Form** customization to display custom survey forms during checkout, allowing the business to collect valuable customer feedback while improving customer engagement and strengthening brand reputation.
</Note>
