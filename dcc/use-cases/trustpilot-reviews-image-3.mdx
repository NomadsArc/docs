How to display **Payment Icons** image on the Checkout using the **Template -> CRO -> Payment Icons** option.

<iframe src="https://www.loom.com/embed/447395b56fc64345adf02cd75cae8bb3" frameborder="0" webkitallowfullscreen mozallowfullscreen allowfullscreen width="100%" height="480" />

## Guide

<Steps>
  <Step title="**Create a new customization**">
    Create a new [customization](https://admin.shopify.com/apps/dynamic-checkout-customizer/customizations).

    ![Image](/images/image-34.png "Image")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Choose the Content type**">
    Select  **Payment icons** from the available **CRO** types to display custom images during checkout.

    ![Template Cro Card Focus](/images/template_cro_card_focus.png "Template Cro Card Focus")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Configure display conditions**">
    Add one or more **conditions** to control when the content appears during checkout.

    ![Payment Icons Conditional Experience Focus](/images/payment_icons_conditional_experience_focus-1.png "Payment Icons Conditional Experience Focus")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Configure the payment method icons**">
    Select the supported payment methods from given options.

    ![Payment Icons Heading Methods Focus](/images/payment_icons_heading_methods_focus.png "Payment Icons Heading Methods Focus")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Activate the customization**">
    Change the **Status** to **Active** using toggle button to enable the customization.

    ![Payment Icons Toolbar Focus](/images/payment_icons_toolbar_focus.png "Payment Icons Toolbar Focus")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Customize the display**">
    Configure the **Display Settings** to control how **payment icons** will appear during checkout.

    ![Payment Icons Display Settings Focus](/images/payment_icons_display_settings_focus.png "Payment Icons Display Settings Focus")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Set the priority**">
    Configure the **Priority** (0–100) to determine the execution order when multiple customizations are eligible.

    ![Payment Icons Priority Focus](/images/payment_icons_priority_focus.png "Payment Icons Priority Focus")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Copy the Placement ID**">
    Copy the generated **Placement ID** from clipboard icon. You'll use this in the Shopify Checkout Editor.

    ![Payment Icons Copy Placement Id Focus](/images/payment_icons_copy_placement_id_focus.png "Payment Icons Copy Placement Id Focus")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Save the customization**">
    Click **Save** to store your configuration.

    ![Payment Icons Unsaved Changes Focus](/images/payment_icons_unsaved_changes_focus.png "Payment Icons Unsaved Changes Focus")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Open the Checkout Editor**">
    Go to your Shopify [Settings](https://admin.shopify.com/settings) **→ Checkout**, select your checkout profile, and click **Edit**.

    ![](/images/screenshot_08.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Add the app block**">
    In the [Checkout Editor](https://admin.shopify.com/settings/checkout/editor), click **Add app block**.

    ![Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 16 25 08](/images/Screenshot-2026-10-08-at-16.25.08.png "Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 16 25 08")

    <Note>
      Make sure the app block is placed in the same checkout placement you selected in Dynamic Checkout Customizer.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Configure the app block**">
    Select the **Checkout – Image Content** app block, and paste the copied **Placement ID**.

    ![Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 16 25 29](/images/Screenshot-2026-10-08-at-16.25.29.png "Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 16 25 29")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Publish your changes**">
    Click **Save** in the Checkout Editor to publish the customization.

    ![](/images/996cbc359c.png)
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  **Why does it matter?**

  [Wellbots-NYC](https://www.wellbots.com/) uses the **Notifications → Content** customization to display Trustpilot review images during checkout, reinforcing social proof and customer trust. Showcasing positive reviews at the final stage of the purchase journey can help increase customer confidence and improve customer acquisition.
</Note>
