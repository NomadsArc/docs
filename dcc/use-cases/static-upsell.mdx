How to Upsell Products on Checkout using the **Custom ->** **Upsell -> Static** option? A simple walkthrough.

<iframe src="https://www.loom.com/embed/ed6fe8f0ab0c474a9aa4bf635a0b37b7" frameborder="0" webkitallowfullscreen mozallowfullscreen allowfullscreen width="100%" height="480" />

## Guide

<Steps>
  <Step title="**Create a new customization**">
    Create a new [customization](https://admin.shopify.com/apps/dynamic-checkout-customizer/customizations).

    ![Image](/images/image-2.png "Image")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Choose the Static Upsell type**">
    Select **Static Source** to manually choose the products you want to recommend during checkout.

    ![Static 1 Upsell Card](/images/static_1_upsell_card.png "Static 1 Upsell Card")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Configure display conditions**">
    Add one or more **conditions** to control when the upsell is displayed during checkout.

    ![Static 2 Conditional Experience](/images/static_2_conditional_experience.png "Static 2 Conditional Experience")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Select upsell products**">
    Manually choose the products you want to display and configure the **Maximum Product Count** shown during checkout.

    ![Static 3 Heading Selection](/images/static_3_heading_selection.png "Static 3 Heading Selection")

    ![Static Source 03](/images/static_source_03.png "Static Source 03")

    ![Static 4 Heading Selection](/images/static_4_heading_selection-2.png "Static 4 Heading Selection")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Customize the display**">
    Configure the **Display Settings** to control how the upsell products appear during checkout.

    ![Static 5 Display Settings](/images/static_5_display_settings.png "Static 5 Display Settings")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Activate the customization**">
    Change the **Status** to **Active** using toggle button to activate the customization.

    ![Image](/images/image-8.png "Image")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Set the priority**">
    Configure the **Priority** (0–100) to determine the execution order when multiple customizations are eligible.

    ![Static 6 Priority](/images/static_6_priority-2.png "Static 6 Priority")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Copy the Placement ID**">
    Copy the generated **Placement ID** from clipboard icon. You'll use this in the Shopify Checkout Editor.

    ![Static 7 Placement Id](/images/static_7_placement_id.png "Static 7 Placement Id")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Preview**">
    Preview shows , How this block looks like at **CheckOut.**

    ![Static Preview Focus](/images/static_preview_focus.png "Static Preview Focus")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Save the customization**">
    Click **Save** to store your configuration.

    ![Static 8 Unsaved Changes](/images/static_8_unsaved_changes-1.png "Static 8 Unsaved Changes")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Open the Checkout Editor**">
    Go to your Shopify [Settings](https://admin.shopify.com/settings)**→ Checkout**, select your checkout profile, and click **Edit**.

    ![](/images/screenshot_08.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Add the app block**">
    In the Checkout Editor, click **Add app block**.

    ![](/images/screenshot_12.png)

    <Note>
      Make sure the app block is placed in the same checkout placement you selected in Dynamic Checkout Customizer.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Configure the app block**">
    Select the **Checkout – Upsell Products** app block,  and paste the copied **Placement ID**.

    ![](/images/screenshot_02.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Publish your changes**">
    Click **Save** in the Checkout Editor to publish the customization.

    ![Screenshot 02 Save Clicked](/images/screenshot_02_save_clicked-1.png "Screenshot 02 Save Clicked")
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  **Why does it matter?**

  [FameOnCentral](https://fameoncentral.com/) uses the **Static Upsell** customization to promote manually selected products during checkout, helping increase average order value (AOV) by encouraging customers to add complementary items before completing their purchase.
</Note>
