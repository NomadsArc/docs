---
title: "Remove Cart Item"
---

This will let the customer to remove cart items during checkout

<iframe src="https://drive.google.com/file/d/1VLz73JBkACvzaKPQkVLnkbmrjHWvVpMu/preview" width="640" height="480" allow="autoplay" />

## Guide

<Steps>
  <Step title="**Create a new customization**">
    Create a new [**customization**](https://admin.shopify.com/apps/dynamic-checkout-customizer/customizations).

    ![](/images/screenshot_13.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Choose the Remove option**">
    Choose the **Remove** option from Cart Item Details section

    ![Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 17 01 06](/images/Screenshot-2026-10-08-at-17.01.06.png "Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 17 01 06")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Configure display condition**">
    Add one or more conditions to control Remove option.

    ![Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 17 01 41](/images/Screenshot-2026-10-08-at-17.01.41.png "Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 17 01 41")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Check Remove Item**">
    Check Remove Item from Action Section to enable the relevant action.

    ![Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 17 01 54](/images/Screenshot-2026-10-08-at-17.01.54.png "Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 17 01 54")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Set the priority**">
    Configure the Priority (0–100) to determine the execution order when multiple customizations are eligible.

    ![Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 17 02 13](/images/Screenshot-2026-10-08-at-17.02.13-2.png "Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 17 02 13")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Copy the Placement ID**">
    Open the **Placement** tab and copy the generated **Placement ID**. You’ll use this in the Shopify Checkout Editor.

    ![Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 17 02 50](/images/Screenshot-2026-10-08-at-17.02.50.png "Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 17 02 50")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Activate the customization**">
    Change the **Status** to **Active** to enable the customization.

    ![Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 17 02 26](/images/Screenshot-2026-10-08-at-17.02.26.png "Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 17 02 26")
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Checkout Editor">
    Go to your Shopify [**Settings**](https://admin.shopify.com/settings)\*\* → Checkout\*\*, select your checkout profile, and click **Customize**.

    ![](/images/screenshot_08.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the app block">
    In the [Checkout Editor](https://admin.shopify.com/settings/checkout/editor), click **Add app block**.

    ![Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 16 32 10](/images/Screenshot-2026-10-08-at-16.32.10.png "Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 16 32 10")

    <Note>
      Make sure the app block is placed in the same checkout placement you selected in Dynamic Checkout Customizer.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure the app block">
    Select the **Checkout – Image Content** app block, drag it to the desired location, and paste the copied **Placement ID**.

    ![Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 16 32 42](/images/Screenshot-2026-10-08-at-16.32.42.png "Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 16 32 42")
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish your changes">
    Click **Save** in the Checkout Editor to publish the customization.

    ![](/images/Screenshot-2026-10-08-at-16.32.42.png)
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  **Why does it matter?**

  Address Validation helps reduce shipping errors by validating customer address input during checkout. This can reduce shipping and logistics costs by **up to 10%** while improving delivery accuracy and customer satisfaction.
</Note>
