---
title: "📑 Display Tags"
---

How to add **Custom** **Tags** for each cart item on the Checkout using the **Cart Item Details -\> Display Tags** option.

<iframe src="https://www.loom.com/embed/e8348fec89c24ee78b3686427e058f31" frameborder="0" webkitallowfullscreen mozallowfullscreen allowfullscreen width="100%" height="480" />

## Guide

<Steps>
  <Step title="**Create a new customization**">
    Create a new [**customization**](https://admin.shopify.com/apps/dynamic-checkout-customizer/customizations).

    ![](/images/screenshot_13.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Choose Display tags from Cart item details section**">
    Choose the Display Tags option from Cart Item Details section

    ![Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 17 20 33](/images/Screenshot-2026-10-08-at-17.20.33.png "Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 17 20 33")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Configure conditional experience**">
    Add one or more conditions to control Display tags.

    ![Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 17 21 04](/images/Screenshot-2026-10-08-at-17.21.04.png "Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 17 21 04")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Edit Cart Items Section**">
    Add one or more products conditons for each cart product in Edit Cart Items 

    ![Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 17 21 33](/images/Screenshot-2026-10-08-at-17.21.33.png "Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 17 21 33")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Display Settings**">
    Choose display setting i.e. (Auto,Neutral,Critical)

    ![Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 17 22 43](/images/Screenshot-2026-10-08-at-17.22.43.png "Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 17 22 43")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Set the priority**">
    Configure the Priority (0–100) to determine the execution order when multiple customizations are eligible.

    ![Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 17 23 37](/images/Screenshot-2026-10-08-at-17.23.37.png "Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 17 23 37")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Copy the Placement ID**">
    Open the **Placement** tab and copy the generated **Placement ID**. You’ll use this in the Shopify Checkout Editor.

    ![Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 17 23 55](/images/Screenshot-2026-10-08-at-17.23.55.png "Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 17 23 55")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Activate the customization**">
    Change the **Status** to **Active** to enable the customization.

    ![Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 17 23 12](/images/Screenshot-2026-10-08-at-17.23.12.png "Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 17 23 12")
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Checkout Editor">
    Go to your Shopify [**Settings**](https://admin.shopify.com/settings)\*\* → Checkout\*\*, select your checkout profile, and click **Customize**.

    ![](/images/screenshot_08.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the app block">
    In the [Checkout Editor](https://admin.shopify.com/settings/checkout/editor), click **Add app block**.

    ![Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 16 32 10](/images/Screenshot-2026-10-08-at-16.32.10.png "Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 16 32 10")

    <Note>
      Make sure the app block is placed in the same checkout placement you selected in Dynamic Checkout Customizer.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure the app block">
    Select the **Checkout – Image Content** app block, drag it to the desired location, and paste the copied **Placement ID**.

    ![Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 16 32 42](/images/Screenshot-2026-10-08-at-16.32.42.png "Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 16 32 42")
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish your changes">
    Click **Save** in the Checkout Editor to publish the customization.

    ![](/images/Screenshot-2026-10-08-at-16.32.42.png)
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  **Why does it matter?**

  [BoutiquesByTheBox](https://boutiquebythebox.com) uses the **Cart Item Details → Display Tags** customization to highlight important product information during checkout, creating a more informative shopping experience that can help increase average order value (AOV).
</Note>
