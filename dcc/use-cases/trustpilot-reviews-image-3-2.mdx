How to display **Trust Badges** on the Checkout using the **Notification -> Content** option.

<iframe src="https://www.loom.com/embed/d23cd7c262854e15843f1574e7e62dc0" frameborder="0" webkitallowfullscreen mozallowfullscreen allowfullscreen width="100%" height="480" />

## Guide

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a new customization">
    Create a new [customization](https://admin.shopify.com/apps/dynamic-checkout-customizer/customizations).

    ![](/images/screenshot_13.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the Content type">
    Select  **Trust Badges **from the available **CRO** types to display custom images during checkout.

    ![Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 19 20 16](/images/Screenshot-2026-10-08-at-19.20.16.png "Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 19 20 16")
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure display conditions">
    Add one or more **conditions** to control when the content appears during checkout.

    ![Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 19 21 39](/images/Screenshot-2026-10-08-at-19.21.39.png "Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 19 21 39")
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure the payment method icons">
    Adjust these settings to match your store's branding and checkout experience.

    ![Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 19 20 58](/images/Screenshot-2026-10-08-at-19.20.58.png "Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 19 20 58")
  </Step>
  <Step title="Activate the customization">
    Change the **Status** to **Active** to enable the customization.

    ![Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 19 21 39](/images/Screenshot-2026-10-08-at-19.21.39-2.png "Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 19 21 39")
  </Step>
  <Step title="Customize the display">
    Configure the **Display Settings** to control how the upsell products appear during checkout.

    ![Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 19 21 10](/images/Screenshot-2026-10-08-at-19.21.10.png "Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 19 21 10")
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the priority">
    Configure the **Priority** (0–100) to determine the execution order when multiple customizations are eligible.

    ![Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 19 21 53](/images/Screenshot-2026-10-08-at-19.21.53.png "Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 19 21 53")
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the Placement ID">
    Open the **Placement** tab and copy the generated **Placement ID**. You'll use this in the Shopify Checkout Editor.

    ![Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 19 22 07](/images/Screenshot-2026-10-08-at-19.22.07.png "Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 19 22 07")
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the customization">
    Click **Save** to store your configuration.

    ![Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 19 21 39](/images/Screenshot-2026-10-08-at-19.21.39-1.png "Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 19 21 39")
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Checkout Editor">
    Go to your Shopify [Settings](https://admin.shopify.com/settings) **→ Checkout**, select your checkout profile, and click **Customize**.

    ![](/images/screenshot_08.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the app block">
    In the [Checkout Editor](https://admin.shopify.com/settings/checkout/editor), click **Add app block**.

    ![Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 16 25 08](/images/Screenshot-2026-10-08-at-16.25.08.png "Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 16 25 08")

    <Note>
      Make sure the app block is placed in the same checkout placement you selected in Dynamic Checkout Customizer.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure the app block">
    Select the **Checkout – Image Content** app block, drag it to the desired location, and paste the copied **Placement ID**.

    ![Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 16 25 29](/images/Screenshot-2026-10-08-at-16.25.29.png "Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 16 25 29")
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish your changes">
    Click **Save** in the Checkout Editor to publish the customization.

    ![](/images/996cbc359c.png)
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  **Why does it matter?**

  [Wellbots-NYC](https://www.wellbots.com/) uses the **Notifications → Content** customization to display Trustpilot review images during checkout, reinforcing social proof and customer trust. Showcasing positive reviews at the final stage of the purchase journey can help increase customer confidence and improve customer acquisition.
</Note>
