How to Dynamically Upsell Products on Checkout using the **Custom ->** **Upsell -> Dynamic** option? A simple walkthrough.

<iframe src="https://www.loom.com/embed/0bf90192cb5c438cadd98311934eb624" frameborder="0" webkitallowfullscreen mozallowfullscreen allowfullscreen width="100%" height="480" />

## Guide

<Steps>
  <Step title="**Create a new customization**">
    Create a new [customization](https://admin.shopify.com/apps/dynamic-checkout-customizer/customizations).

    ![Image](/images/image-13.png "Image")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Choose the Dynamic Source from Upsell type**">
    Select **Dynamic Source** to recommend products automatically using Shopify search and discovery app.

    ![Upsell Focus](/images/upsell_focus-2.png "Upsell Focus")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Configure display conditions**">
    Add one or more **conditions** to control when the upsell is displayed during checkout.

    ![Conditional Experience Focus](/images/conditional_experience_focus.png "Conditional Experience Focus")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Configure the dynamic source**">
    Enter the **Heading  **that will display over your recommended/complementary products. Then configure:

    - **Max Recommendations (Count)**
    - **Recommended For (product)**
    - **Dynamic Conditions**

    ![Dynamic Settings Focus](/images/dynamic_settings_focus.png "Dynamic Settings Focus")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Customize the display**">
    Configure the **Display Settings** to control how the upsell products appear during checkout.

    ![Display Settings Focus](/images/display_settings_focus.png "Display Settings Focus")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Activate the customization**">
    Change the **Status** to **Active** using toggle button to activate the customization.

    ![Toolbar Focus](/images/toolbar_focus.png "Toolbar Focus")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Set the priority**">
    Configure the **Priority** (0–100) to determine the execution order when multiple customizations are eligible.

    ![Priority Focus](/images/priority_focus.png "Priority Focus")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Copy the Placement ID**">
    Copy the generated **Placement ID** from clipboard icon. You'll use this in the Shopify Checkout Editor.

    ![Copy Placement Id Focus](/images/copy_placement_id_focus.png "Copy Placement Id Focus")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Save the customization**">
    Click **Save** to store your configuration.

    ![Unsaved Changes Focus](/images/unsaved_changes_focus.png "Unsaved Changes Focus")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Open the Checkout Editor**">
    Go to your Shopify [Settings](https://admin.shopify.com/settings)**→ Checkout**, select your checkout profile, and click **Edit**.

    ![Screenshot 08](/images/screenshot_08.png "Screenshot 08")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Add the app block**">
    In the Checkout Editor, click **Add app block**.

    ![Screenshot 12](/images/screenshot_12.png "Screenshot 12")

    <Note>
      Make sure the app block is placed in the same checkout placement you selected in Dynamic Checkout Customizer.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Configure the app block**">
    Select the **Checkout – Upsell Products** app block,  and paste the copied **Placement ID**.

    ![Screenshot 02](/images/screenshot_02.png "Screenshot 02")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Publish your changes**">
    Click **Save** in the Checkout Editor to publish the customization.

    ![Screenshot 02 Save Clicked](/images/screenshot_02_save_clicked.png "Screenshot 02 Save Clicked")
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  **Why does it matter?**

  [FameOnCentral](https://fameoncentral.com/) uses the **Dynamic Upsell** customization to recommend relevant products during checkout, helping increase average order value (AOV) by presenting personalized cross-sell opportunities at the right moment.
</Note>
