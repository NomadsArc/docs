How to **Add Notes** for each cart item on the Checkout using the **Cart Item Details -\> Add Notes** option.

<iframe src="https://www.loom.com/embed/7d714e0ee3224058b51b15d39a034772" frameborder="0" webkitallowfullscreen mozallowfullscreen allowfullscreen width="100%" height="480" />

## Guide

<Steps>
  <Step title="**Create a new customization**">
    Create a new [customization](https://admin.shopify.com/apps/dynamic-checkout-customizer/customizations).

    ![](/images/screenshot_13.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select **Add Notes** from Cart Item Details category">
    Choose **Add Notes** as the customization category.

    ![Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 17 18 53](/images/Screenshot-2026-10-08-at-17.18.53.png "Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 17 18 53")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Configure conditions**">
    Add one or more **conditions** to control when the customization is displayed during checkout.

    ![Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 17 15 15](/images/Screenshot-2026-10-08-at-17.15.15.png "Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 17 15 15")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Configure note settings**">
    Customize the **Link Title**, **Notes Title**, and **Notes Key** for each cart item.

    ![Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 17 16 13](/images/Screenshot-2026-10-08-at-17.16.13-1.png "Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 17 16 13")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Add Display Settings**">
    Add Character Counts from Display Settings 

    ![Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 17 17 36](/images/Screenshot-2026-10-08-at-17.17.36.png "Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 17 17 36")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Set the priority**">
    Configure the **Priority** (0–100) to determine the execution order when multiple customizations are eligible.

    ![Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 17 18 32](/images/Screenshot-2026-10-08-at-17.18.32.png "Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 17 18 32")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Copy the Placement ID**">
    Open the **Placement** tab and copy the generated **Placement ID**. You'll use this in the Shopify Checkout Editor.

    ![Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 17 56 01](/images/Screenshot-2026-10-08-at-17.56.01.png "Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 17 56 01")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Save the customization**">
    Click **Save** to store your configuration.

    ![](/images/8404816493.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Activate the customization**">
    Change the **Status** to **Active** to enable the customization.

    ![Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 17 07 39](/images/Screenshot-2026-10-08-at-17.07.39.png "Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 17 07 39")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Open the Checkout Editor**">
    Go to your Shopify [**Settings**](https://admin.shopify.com/settings)\*\* → Checkout\*\*, select your checkout profile, and click **Customize**.

    ![](/images/screenshot_08.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Add the app block**">
    In the [Checkout Editor](https://admin.shopify.com/settings/checkout/editor), click **Add app block**.

    ![Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 16 32 10](/images/Screenshot-2026-10-08-at-16.32.10.png "Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 16 32 10")

    <Note>
      Make sure the app block is placed in the same checkout placement you selected in Dynamic Checkout Customizer.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Configure the app block**">
    Select the **Checkout – Image Content** app block, drag it to the desired location, and paste the copied **Placement ID**.

    ![Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 16 32 42](/images/Screenshot-2026-10-08-at-16.32.42.png "Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 16 32 42")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Publish your changes**">
    Click **Save** in the Checkout Editor to publish the customization.

    ![](/images/Screenshot-2026-10-08-at-16.32.42.png)
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Why does it matter?

  Cart Item Details -\> Add Notes customization for adding custom notes can improve AOV by 10%.
</Note>
