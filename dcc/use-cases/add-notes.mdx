How to **Add Notes** for each cart item on the Checkout using the **Custom ->** **Cart Item Details -> Add Notes** option.

## Guide

<Steps>
  <Step title="**Create a new customization**">
    Create a new [customization](https://admin.shopify.com/apps/dynamic-checkout-customizer/customizations).

    ![Image](/images/image-41.png "Image")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Select Add Notes from Cart Item Details category**">
    Choose Add tags as the customization from available types of Cart Item Detail.

    ![Custom Cart Item Details Card Add Notes Focus](/images/custom_cart_item_details_card_add_notes_focus.png "Custom Cart Item Details Card Add Notes Focus")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Configure conditions**">
    Add one or more **conditions** to control when the customization is displayed during checkout.

    ![Add Notes Conditional Experience Focus](/images/add_notes_conditional_experience_focus.png "Add Notes Conditional Experience Focus")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Configure note settings**">
    Customize the **Link Title**, **Notes Title**, and **Notes Key** for each cart item.

    ![Add Notes Notes Settings Focus](/images/add_notes_notes_settings_focus.png "Add Notes Notes Settings Focus")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Add Display Settings**">
    Add Character Counts from Display Settings 

    ![Add Notes Display Settings Focus](/images/add_notes_display_settings_focus.png "Add Notes Display Settings Focus")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Set the priority**">
    Configure the **Priority** (0–100) to determine the execution order when multiple customizations are eligible.

    ![Add Notes Priority Focus](/images/add_notes_priority_focus.png "Add Notes Priority Focus")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Copy the Placement ID**">
    Copy the generated **Placement ID** from clipboard icon. You'll use this in the Shopify Checkout Editor.

    ![Add Notes Copy Placement Id Focus](/images/add_notes_copy_placement_id_focus.png "Add Notes Copy Placement Id Focus")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Preview**">
    Preview shows , How this block looks like at **CheckOut.**

    ![Add Notes Preview Focus](/images/add_notes_preview_focus.png "Add Notes Preview Focus")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Save the customization**">
    Click **Save** to store your configuration.

    ![Add Notes Unsaved Changes Focus](/images/add_notes_unsaved_changes_focus.png "Add Notes Unsaved Changes Focus")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Activate the customization**">
    Change the **Status** to **Active** using toggle button to enable the customization.

    ![Add Notes Toolbar Focus](/images/add_notes_toolbar_focus.png "Add Notes Toolbar Focus")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Open the Checkout Editor**">
    Go to your Shopify [**Settings**](https://admin.shopify.com/settings) → Checkout, select your checkout profile, and click **Edit**.

    ![](/images/screenshot_08.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Add the app block**">
    In the [Checkout Editor](https://admin.shopify.com/settings/checkout/editor), click **Add app block**.

    ![Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 16 32 10](/images/Screenshot-2026-10-08-at-16.32.10.png "Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 16 32 10")

    <Note>
      Make sure the app block is placed in the same checkout placement you selected in Dynamic Checkout Customizer.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Configure the app block**">
    Select the **Checkout – Edit cart items** app block, and paste the copied **Placement ID**.

    ![Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 16 32 42](/images/Screenshot-2026-10-08-at-16.32.42.png "Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 16 32 42")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Publish your changes**">
    Click **Save** in the Checkout Editor to publish the customization.

    ![Swap Clean](/images/swap_clean-3.png "Swap Clean")
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Why does it matter?

  Cart Item Details -\> Add Notes customization for adding custom notes can improve AOV by 10%.
</Note>
