title: "🔢 Line Item Editor"
---

How to display a **Quantity Selector** for each cart item on the Checkout using the **Template/Custom ->** **Cart Item Details -> Line item editor** option.

<iframe src="https://www.loom.com/embed/450773eaed82428d8c238a73b8ba3bbe" frameborder="0" webkitallowfullscreen mozallowfullscreen allowfullscreen width="100%" height="480" />

## Guide

<Steps>
  <Step title="**Create a new customization**">
    Create a new [customization](https://admin.shopify.com/apps/dynamic-checkout-customizer/customizations).

    ![Image](/images/image-40.png "Image")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Select the Cart Item Details category**">
    Choose Line Item Editor as the customization from available types of Cart Item Detail.

    ![Custom Cart Item Details Card Focus](/images/custom_cart_item_details_card_focus.png "Custom Cart Item Details Card Focus")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Configure display conditions**">
    Add one or more **conditions** to control when the quantity selector is displayed during checkout.

    ![Line Item Conditional Experience Focus](/images/line_item_conditional_experience_focus.png "Line Item Conditional Experience Focus")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Configure Line Item Actions**">
    Click on Quantity Stepper checkbox to configure relevant action.

    ![Line Item Actions Focus](/images/line_item_actions_focus.png "Line Item Actions Focus")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Set the priority**">
    Configure the **Priority** (0–100) to determine the execution order when multiple customizations are eligible.

    ![Line Item Priority Focus](/images/line_item_priority_focus.png "Line Item Priority Focus")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Activate the customization**">
    Change the **Status** to **Active** using toggle button to enable the customization.

    ![Line Item Toolbar Focus](/images/line_item_toolbar_focus.png "Line Item Toolbar Focus")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Copy the Placement ID**">
    Copy the generated **Placement ID** from clipboard icon. You'll use this in the Shopify Checkout Editor.

    ![Line Item Copy Placement Id Focus](/images/line_item_copy_placement_id_focus.png "Line Item Copy Placement Id Focus")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Save the customization**">
    Click **Save** to store your configuration.

    ![Line Item Unsaved Changes Focus](/images/line_item_unsaved_changes_focus.png "Line Item Unsaved Changes Focus")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Open the Checkout Editor**">
    Go to your Shopify [**Settings**](https://admin.shopify.com/settings) → Checkout, select your checkout profile, and click **Edit**.

    ![](/images/screenshot_08.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Add the app block**">
    In the [Checkout Editor](https://admin.shopify.com/settings/checkout/editor), click **Add app block**.

    ![Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 16 32 10](/images/Screenshot-2026-10-08-at-16.32.10.png "Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 16 32 10")

    <Note>
      Make sure the app block is placed in the same checkout placement you selected in Dynamic Checkout Customizer.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Configure the app block**">
    Select the **Checkout – Edit cart item** app block, and paste the copied **Placement ID**.

    ![Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 16 32 42](/images/Screenshot-2026-10-08-at-16.32.42.png "Screenshot 2026 10 08 At 16 32 42")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Publish your changes**">
    Click **Save** in the Checkout Editor to publish the customization.

    ![](/images/Screenshot-2026-10-08-at-16.32.42.png)
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  **Why does it matter?**

  The **Cart Item Details → Adjust Quantity** customization lets customers update product quantities directly during checkout, reducing friction and making it easier to increase order quantities without returning to the cart. This can help improve average order value (AOV).
</Note>
