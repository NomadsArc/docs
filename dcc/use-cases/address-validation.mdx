How to validate the **Address lines** on checkout page?

<iframe src="https://drive.google.com/file/d/1VLz73JBkACvzaKPQkVLnkbmrjHWvVpMu/preview" width="640" height="480" allow="autoplay" />

## Guide

<Steps>
  <Step title="**Open Checkout Validation**">
    Go to **Checkout Validation** page.

    ![Image](/images/image-49.png "Image")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Create Validation**">
    Click create validation button.

    ![Image](/images/image-50.png "Image")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Configure conditional experience**">
    Add one or more **conditions** to control when the validation is displayed during checkout.

    ![Image](/images/image-51.png "Image")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Add validation Category**">
    Select a validation Rule according to your need.

    ![Image](/images/image-52.png "Image")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Activate the validation**">
    Change the **Status** to **Active** using toggle button to enable the validation.

    ![Image](/images/image-53.png "Image")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Save the customization**">
    Click Save to keep your configuration.

    ![Image](/images/image-54.png "Image")
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  **Why does it matter?**

  Checkout Validation helps reduce shipping errors by validating customer address input during checkout. This can reduce shipping and logistics costs by **up to 10%** while improving delivery accuracy and customer satisfaction.
</Note>
