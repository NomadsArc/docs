How to display **Shipping banners** on Checkout using the **Template ->** **Content -> Shipping delay alert** option? A Step by Step Guide.

<iframe src="https://www.loom.com/embed/23c1d08387994e2fbdfe76bc69f4e429" frameborder="0" webkitallowfullscreen mozallowfullscreen allowfullscreen width="100%" height="480" />

## Guide

<Steps>
  <Step title="**Create a new customization**">
    Create a new [customization](https://admin.shopify.com/apps/dynamic-checkout-customizer/customizations).

    ![Image](/images/image-33.png "Image")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Choose the Banner type**">
    Select **Shipping delay alert **from the available content types to display informational or promotional banners during checkout.

    ![Template Content Card Focus](/images/template_content_card_focus.png "Template Content Card Focus")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Configure display conditions**">
    Add one or more **conditions** to control when the banner is displayed during checkout.

    ![Shipping Delay Conditional Experience Focus](/images/shipping_delay_conditional_experience_focus.png "Shipping Delay Conditional Experience Focus")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Configure the banner**">
    Customize the banner by adding **Title** and **Description **that customers will see during checkout.

    ![Shipping Delay Alert Settings Focus](/images/shipping_delay_alert_settings_focus.png "Shipping Delay Alert Settings Focus")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Activate the customization**">
    Change the **Status** to **Active** using toggle button to activate the customization.

    ![Shipping Delay Toolbar Focus](/images/shipping_delay_toolbar_focus.png "Shipping Delay Toolbar Focus")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Set the priority**">
    Configure the **Priority** (0–100) to determine the execution order when multiple customizations are eligible.

    ![Shipping Delay Priority Focus](/images/shipping_delay_priority_focus.png "Shipping Delay Priority Focus")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Copy the Placement ID**">
    Copy the generated **Placement ID** from clipboard icon** **. You'll use this in the Shopify Checkout Editor.

    ![Shipping Delay Copy Placement Id Focus](/images/shipping_delay_copy_placement_id_focus.png "Shipping Delay Copy Placement Id Focus")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Save the customization**">
    Click **Save** to store your configuration.

    ![Shipping Delay Unsaved Changes Focus](/images/shipping_delay_unsaved_changes_focus.png "Shipping Delay Unsaved Changes Focus")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Open the Checkout Editor**">
    Go to your Shopify [Settings](https://admin.shopify.com/settings)**→ Checkout**, select your checkout profile, and click **Edit**.

    ![](/images/screenshot_08.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Add the app block**">
    In the Checkout Editor, click **Add app block**.

    ![Image (4)](/images/image-\(4\).png "Image (4)")

    <Note>
      Make sure the app block is placed in the same checkout placement you selected in Dynamic Checkout Customizer.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Configure the app block**">
    Select the **Checkout – Instruction Banner** app block, and paste the copied **Placement ID**.

    ![Image (5)](/images/image-\(5\).png "Image (5)")
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Publish your changes**">
    Click **Save** in the Checkout Editor to publish the customization.

    ![](/images/de49128f10.png)
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  **Why does it matter?**

  [Balardi](https://balardi.com/) uses the **Banner Notifications** customization to display important messages and promotional information during checkout, helping improve customer confidence and contributing to higher conversion rates.
</Note>
